### Clinical Survival Analysis: Lung Cancer Outcomes in Veteran Patients
Analysis of a randomized controlled clinical trial from the Veterans Administration lung cancer study. The aim was to assess the efficacy of a new test treatment against a standard treatment. The analysis uses Kaplan-Meier estimation alongside a multivariate Cox Proportional Hazards model.
